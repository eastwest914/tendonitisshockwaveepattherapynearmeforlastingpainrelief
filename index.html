Frequently Asked Questions

Shockwave therapy uses acoustic waves applied to the painful area to help stimulate healing in tendons and soft tissue. It is a non-invasive option often used for chronic tendonitis that has not improved with rest or other care.

EPAT (extracorporeal pulse activation technology) is a form of shockwave treatment commonly used for heel pain, plantar fasciitis, tennis elbow, shoulder pain and other tendon injuries.

Most patients feel pulsing pressure or mild discomfort during a session, which is usually well tolerated. Your clinician can adjust the intensity to keep you comfortable.
